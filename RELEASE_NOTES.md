# V6.3.3 Release Notes — Failed-Thesis Episode Reset

V6.3.3 prevents repeated same-direction entries after failed-thesis exits,
adds visible process-session shadow P/L, and isolates model training and lane
metrics by strategy ID. V6.3.2 conservative fill accounting remains unchanged.

## V6.3.2

V6.3.2 corrects optimistic shadow accounting discovered in a live MT5 shadow session. When a polling cycle first observes price beyond a profitable target, the completed trade is now booked at the planned target barrier rather than at the later quote.

## Added

- planned-target fill accounting for profitable triple-barrier exits;
- observed executable quote and observed market P/L retained in every closed record;
- explicit `barrier_overshoot_usd` diagnostics;
- adverse hard-stop gaps remain charged at the observed quote;
- tests covering profitable target gaps and adverse stop gaps;
- strategy/settings version bump, which invalidates older approvals and model artifacts.

## Unchanged

- STRICT entry thresholds;
- four-lane shadow architecture;
- calibrated learning gates;
- one position and no pyramiding;
- controlled demo remains approval-locked;
- real-account execution remains disabled.

# V6.3.0 Release Notes — Calibrated Self-Learning

## Added

- Fourth research-only `LEARNED` shadow lane.
- Calibrated champion/challenger model registry.
- Logistic, balanced-logistic and random-forest candidates.
- Purged chronological walk-forward evaluation.
- OOS promotion target: win rate >=59%, expectancy >=+$0.03 and profit factor >=1.15.
- Brier-score and expected-calibration-error requirements.
- Global and sufficiently sampled regime-specific probability thresholds.
- Model expiry, settings binding and multivariate PSI drift guard.
- Adaptive spread/ATR target-stop planning with minimum 0.80 payoff ratio.
- Two-stage profit giveback protection.
- Model status and self-learning review commands.

## Leakage and feedback-loop protection

- LEARNED outcomes are excluded from future model training.
- Simultaneous cross-lane simulations are de-correlated into one market episode.
- Overlapping trade outcomes are purged between train and test folds.
- No synthetic data is generated by runtime training.

## Deployment controls

- LEARNED, BALANCED and EXPLORATORY remain permanently shadow-only.
- STRICT demo execution requires both the original approval and an approved V6.3 champion.
- Real-account execution remains disabled.

## Interpretation

The release improves methodology; it does not claim the 59% target has already been achieved. The bot must collect new independent shadow outcomes before a model can be trained or approved.
