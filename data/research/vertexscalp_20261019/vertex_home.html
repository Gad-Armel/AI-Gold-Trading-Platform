01 — The basics

What’s an AI trading bot?

A trading bot is a program that watches the market and places trades for you, following rules written into its code. It reads price data, decides whether conditions match its strategy, then opens and closes positions on your account without you clicking anything.

“AI” usually means the bot adapts its decisions to market conditions instead of firing on a single fixed signal. It does not mean it can see the future. A bot is a tool that applies a strategy consistently. It cannot remove the risk of trading.

A scalping bot focuses on short trades: small moves, held for minutes, repeated many times a day. That style needs speed and discipline, which is exactly what a bot is good at.

  1. It watchesPrice data, spreads and timing, all day, without getting tired.
  2. It decidesCompares what it sees with its rules and your risk settings.
  3. It actsOpens, manages and closes trades on your broker account automatically.
